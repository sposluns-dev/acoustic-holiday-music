An eclectic ensemble based out of Toronto, The Kitchen Sink lives up to its name by incorporating genres related genres: Swing, Country, Jazz, Celtic, Old-Time, and Bluegrass to name a few.

Drawing inspiration from artists such as David Grisman, John Reischman, and Béla Fleck, 
The Kitchen Sink fuses these sonic landscapes through meticulous orchestrations of traditional and contemporary pieces.

Instrumentation: trombone, upright bass, mandolin, guitar, banjo, violin, lap steel guitar.
